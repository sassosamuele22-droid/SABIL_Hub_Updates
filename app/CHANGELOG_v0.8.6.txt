SABIL GR Hub v0.8.6

- Rebranded the desktop app from SABIL Hub to SABIL GR Hub.
- Replaced dark/light mode branding with the new SABIL GR Simracing Team logos.
- Updated Windows app icon and installer branding.
- Reworked the visual identity around the new black/white team colours.
- Added Race performance mode (enabled by default).
- Race performance mode reduces nonessential background polling and UI effects.
- Heavy team/result refreshes are skipped while the app is hidden and reduced while driving.
- Electron background throttling is explicitly enabled and the local app/backend process uses below-normal priority.
- Chat, voice, calls and notifications remain available while Race performance mode is enabled.
