SABIL GR Hub v0.9.5

VOICE FIXES
- Restored the correct room model:
  - normal/custom voice channels (Lobby, Race Room, Strategy Room, etc.) are public to all active team members
  - line-up voice rooms are private to assigned drivers and management
- Fixed unreliable WebRTC audio by queueing ICE candidates until the remote SDP is ready.
- Added automatic ICE restart when a peer connection fails.
- Remote audio explicitly starts after a track is attached.
- Microphone capture now requests echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain control.
- Per-member volume remains adjustable from 0% to 200%.
- Per-member volume is now persisted by user account in local storage and survives reconnects/app restarts.
- Existing active-speaker green ring remains enabled.
- Existing ghost-audio cleanup remains enabled.

No database migration required.
